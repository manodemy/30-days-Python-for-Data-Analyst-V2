---

### 🧠 HOW YOUR SENIOR ANALYST WILL DIAGNOSE YOUR REPORT:

When you send this report, I will run these 4 diagnostic health-checks:
1. **The Hook Health Check (CTR):** If CTR < 1.2%, your thumbnail or first 3 seconds of the reel are losing people. We tweak the hook.
2. **The Traffic Efficiency Check (CPC):** If CPC > ₹18, your audience is too narrow or fatigued. We broaden interests.
3. **The Offer Conversion Check (Lead/Chat Rate):** If clicks are high but WhatsApp chats/checkouts are low, your landing page headline or WhatsApp greeting is not compelling.
4. **The Closing Rate Check (CPA & ROAS):** If CPA ≤ ₹300, we prepare the ad set for a 20% budget scale-up.
